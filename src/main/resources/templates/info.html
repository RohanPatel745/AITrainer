Instructions

Maze Escape:

Train the model to complete the maze in the shortest amount of steps. If it hasn't found the solution within 2000 training cycles, it will stop. The X's are penalty squares which the model is supposed to avoid and the blue squares are penalty blocks that move horizontally. Hint: To make the model take the shortest path, penalize movement in general by adding a negative number.

Dot Collector:

Train the model to collect the most amount of dots in 30 seconds. The position of the dot is completely random. Hint: To make the model move efficiently, make the penalty for moving farther larger than the reward for moving closer to the dot.

Track Challenge:

Train the model to drive the track that is drawn. To draw the track, make sure that it is completely enclosed by gray. Then, draw the goal area. If the car touches anywhere here, it is considered that it finished the track. The goal point is used for reward calculation and should be inside the goal area. This is a single point for it to aim toward. Add main rewards to train the model to complete the track; optimization rewards are not necessary.
